# Governance

OpenT1D is currently a founder-maintained open-source research project. Its governance should make responsibility and decisions visible while allowing the work to move quickly.

## Current maintainer

**Matthias van der Galiën**, [@matthias-van-der-galien](https://github.com/matthias-van-der-galien), is the initial project maintainer. The maintainer is responsible for direction, integration, releases, access management and enforcing project policies.

There is currently no steering council, scientific advisory board or institutional endorsement. The role of maintainer does not imply expertise in every biological area represented by the long-term vision.

## Decisions

Anyone can propose a change, review a contribution or challenge an assumption. Discussions normally take place in issues and pull requests so that the reasoning remains available to future contributors.

Routine changes are decided in their pull request. A decision affecting shared interfaces, scientific conventions, substantial dependencies, licensing or project direction is captured in a short [decision record](docs/decisions/README.md). Record alternatives, consequences and evidence that would justify revisiting it.

The maintainer makes the final integration decision after considering relevant feedback. Unresolved objections should be summarized rather than silently discarded. A contributor can ask for reconsideration with new evidence or a concrete alternative. There is no compulsory waiting period for every change.

## Scientific responsibility

The author of a model or experiment is responsible for its description and evidence. Reviewers should state the scope of their review. Reproducing upstream output, inspecting code and assessing a biological interpretation are different contributions.

External scientific review is sought for claims that depend on specialized domain knowledge. Until it is available, report that limitation and keep the claim at the level the evidence supports. A passing software check does not grant a model a general validation status.

## Maintenance and continuity

Additional maintainers can be appointed after sustained, useful contributions and demonstrated judgment. Record their identity, responsibilities and relevant access here when appointed. Grant only the access required for the role and remove access when it is no longer needed.

Before a sustained absence, the maintainer should document project status and arrange a willing replacement if available. If maintenance stops without a successor, state that clearly in the README and preserve releases and research records. Contributors retain the right to fork under the project license.

The current single-maintainer arrangement cannot provide an independent internal appeal panel. Do not imply that one exists. Add an independent conduct contact when a willing, suitable person is appointed.

## Conflicts and funding

Disclose financial or institutional interests relevant to a model, benchmark or decision under discussion. Funding does not confer scientific endorsement or automatic control over results. A person with a material conflict should avoid being the sole reviewer where another qualified reviewer is available; otherwise record the conflict and review limitation.

Governance changes follow the same public decision process as other project-wide decisions.
