# 0036 — Retire the present C1 feedback lead and preflight a comparator

Status: accepted locally on 26 September 2026 for OT-019 development.

## Context

The [C1/0.9 target-alignment round](../research/c1-target-alignment-results.md)
completed all 48 planned native cases but failed the fixed B-benefit and
A-preservation gates in adult#001/102. The [C1/1.0 cross-round review](../research/c1-method-comparator-review.md)
verifies both complete matrices and identical M1 base traces. Lowering the
feedback target improved the failing cells but left both gate failures.
Both declared setting attempts per feedback method are spent. No M1 strong
published feedback comparator is yet qualified under the C1 input and
action contract.

## Decision

Retire the current two-sided PI-plus-rise feedback family as the lead for
the missing-announcement benefit claim. Preserve the kernel, zero-rise
ablation, native attempts and negative outcomes. Do not search a third
target/gain setting on the same known cells under the spent budget. Do not
select a replacement original controller from these trajectories alone.

Make a source-pinned OpenAPS `oref0` **feasibility preflight** the next C1
implementation package. It is a candidate published reference, not an
eligible strong comparator yet. Require source/license and upstream-example
verification, causal input mapping, M1 clock/action fidelity, failure
behavior and matched development-budget rules before comparative native
scoring. If it cannot meet these constraints, retain that finding and
assess the next sourced reference. Keep LT1 Cambridge MPC in its own
model-family track.

## Alternatives, consequences and revisit rule

A third PI target or gain attempt would consume the same known evidence
without repairing its failed predeclared gate. Immediate selection of an
insulin-action-aware original method would lack a specified source,
state/action contract and strong comparator. Treat that family as a
hypothesis for later assessment. Revisit the method choice only after a
newly declared mechanism and setting budget; revisit published-comparator
eligibility only after the source and adapter preflight passes. Any later
evaluation requires a separate exact freeze and genuinely new assessment
material. This decision makes no treatment or human-performance claim.
