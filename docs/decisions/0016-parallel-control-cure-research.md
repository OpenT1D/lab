# 0016 — Parallel glucose-control and cure-oriented research

Status: superseded for future priorities and mandatory original-method objectives by [decision 0045](0045-tool-first-research-platform.md) on 26 September 2026. The parallel application areas and separate evidence gates remain; this historical decision did not change the implementation or evidence status of any scientific capability.

## Context

The laboratory has bounded glucose-control experiments and synthetic forecast preparation. The maintainer wants cure-oriented research to progress alongside that work. M4/M5 previously allowed independent entry but appeared under longer-term research, without immediate deliverables. That ordering could keep biological preparation deferred behind controller development.

## Decision

Keep the [roadmap](../research/roadmap-history-2026-10-28.md#development-sequence-after-m2) canonical. Give glucose regulation (M3/C1/B1/V1) and cure-oriented biology (M4/M5) explicit parallel preparation priorities, using the existing laboratory where suitable. This amends the priority ordering in [decision 0006](0006-model-controller-development.md), preserving its evidence requirements and historical protocols.

Begin biological preparation with OT-024 question/evidence feasibility and OT-015 secretion specification. OT-025 reproduces a standalone functional reference; OT-026 couples and assesses it. OT-027 prepares one cell- or immune-therapy question in parallel, allowing its measurement needs to inform model selection. Therapy execution depends on the mechanisms and observations required by that question; it does not require an entire immune-system model or completion of a new controller.

Reuse provenance, data boundaries, numerical checks and report reconstruction. Reuse metabolic components only after assessing the specific coupling. Keep functional secretion distinct from anatomical cell mass, endogenous delivery distinct from subcutaneous dosing, and numerical verification distinct from biological or intervention evidence. A secretion scenario or accurate glucose forecast does not establish a cure or therapy effect.

At each bounded-deliverable review, make the next artifact, owner when assigned, evidence status and blocker visible for both tracks. Parallel priorities do not imply additional staffing: if only one implementation can proceed, explicitly alternate bounded deliverables or record a reason to defer a question. P1 follows a concrete supported user workflow, not a requirement to complete both scientific tracks.

## Alternatives

- Finish controller development before biology: delays evidence and partnership preparation without a scientific dependency.
- Abandon glucose regulation: discards useful research and shared metabolic infrastructure.
- Build a comprehensive cure simulator immediately: introduces mechanisms before there is a bounded question, suitable measurements or an assessment plan.

## Consequences and revisit conditions

M4/M5 remain planned; this decision creates no biological module, data access, partnership or treatment result. Evidence requirements and independent M2 reproduction remain unchanged. Data-access applications and messages to potential partners require separate authorization; preparing a scientific brief does not send it.

Revisit a question when candidate observations cannot support it, model parameters cannot be distinguished from one another, source rights prevent reproduction, domain review challenges the mechanism, or capacity requires rescheduling. Record narrowed scope and negative findings rather than treating a synthetic scenario as empirical validation. No timeline for a cure or clinical use follows from this roadmap.
