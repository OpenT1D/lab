# 0028 — Conditional nuisance-reoptimized likelihood profiles

Status: accepted on 22 September 2026 before implementation or fitting, starting from `da5adbf` in the existing isolated worktree. Acceptance specifies the method; it does not establish completed execution or biological validity.

BC-012 showed that accurate noiseless recovery and full local sensitivity rank can coexist with substantial noisy parameter errors. The next bounded OT-026 contribution is therefore the [BC-013 profile experiment](../research/m4-profile-likelihood-protocol.md), preserving the forward model and examining uncertainty conditional on its assumptions. Use the known 30-minute development examples, correct fixed-volume GIC, free-volume GIC and a matched GI control. Across the noiseless and two existing noise cases, profile all observable estimated parameters: 27 curves from nine design/data combinations. This deliberately excludes a new sampling comparison, population calibration and human confidence claims.

At each interest-parameter value, refit every other unknown within the existing engineering bounds. [Raue et al. 2009](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btp358), section 4, equation 10, defines this nuisance reoptimization; section 2.1, equations 4–6, connects fixed-error Gaussian likelihood and weighted least squares. A slice with nuisance parameters fixed at their best-fit values is retained only as a diagnostic comparator. Local Hessian intervals or more synthetic point estimates would not answer the chosen question.

Eliminate a free nuisance volume analytically because the unchanged observation equation is linear in inverse volume. For candidate rate/gain parameters, minimizing the C-peptide quadratic gives `a=clip(sum(q*y)/sum(q*q),1/1.5,1/0.5)` and `volume_scale=1/a`, where q is concentration at volume one. Keep its estimate and bound hits. Numerically refit the remaining nuisance coordinates; do not project when volume itself is fixed or profiled. This is exact conditional minimization, not a fixed-volume assumption. Verify it independently and compare unrestricted optima with the unchanged BC-012 numerical-volume implementation.

Exclude exact t=0 G/I from the stochastic likelihood while retaining basal Cp. Condition explicitly on basal G/I, known SC input, equilibrium initialization and all non-varied physiology. Use declared fixed Gaussian SDs and `Q=2*least_squares.cost`; do not rescale residuals to estimate an error variance. The 3.8414588 contour is a nominal asymptotic one-parameter reference. [Self and Liang 1987](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1987.10478472), section 3, pp. 607–609, and section 4, p. 610, support preserving limitations from boundary and nearby nuisance estimates. No simultaneous, finite-sample or human coverage is claimed.

Retain all starts, coarse/midpoint scans, every detected crossing, bisection history, solver refits and failures. Refine all crossing brackets and check reference-solver objectives and contour sides using that solver's own unrestricted minimum. A bound-limited region remains bound-limited, and a sampled curve does not exclude unseen disconnected components. Reject incomplete or failed campaigns and profiles inconsistent with the baseline or feasible fixed-nuisance slice. Preserve historical references and reconstruct reports from source-bound artifacts, with a complete fresh-process repeat.

Revisit this decision if results require a wider parameter domain, more nuisance physiology, different noise assumptions or a coverage question.
