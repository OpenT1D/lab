# BC-014 — Independent synthetic coverage assessment

Specified on 24 September 2026 before implementation or assessment draws, starting from `968fdfa` on local `main`. The [machine protocol](../../experiments/m4/coverage/protocol.json), BC-014/1, is the executable specification. [Decision 0029](../decisions/0029-independent-synthetic-coverage.md) records why this assessment follows BC-013. Neither the [BC-013 protocol](m4-profile-likelihood-protocol.md) nor the BC-012/BC-011 forward model changes.

## Question and permitted conclusion

For new synthetic Gaussian observation errors under the existing model, how often does the **nominal pointwise gain profile** include its generating gain? [BC-013](m4-profile-likelihood-results.md) used only two known development draws and did not assess coverage. This experiment can estimate conditional synthetic inclusion frequencies for the declared patient, model, noise, bounds and starts. It cannot establish human confidence-interval coverage, T1D biological validity, global identifiability or a treatment effect.

The inherited weighted objective is `Q=sum(((prediction-observation)/fixed_SD)^2)`. Under the declared independent Gaussian errors, this is `-2 log L` up to a constant. [Raue et al. 2009](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btp358), sections 2.1–2.2 and 4, motivates the nuisance-reoptimized pointwise profile and nominal `DeltaQ=3.841458820694124` reference. [Self and Liang 1987](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1987.10478472), sections 3–4, motivates separating the gain-boundary diagnostic because standard reference distributions can fail at boundaries. We assess the procedure empirically under its own synthetic assumptions; neither source supplies coverage results for this model.

## Frozen design

The primary interior generating vector is `[1,1,1,1]` for evoked gain, peripheral insulin removal, C-peptide removal and central C-peptide volume multipliers. Generate **64 new, distinct** Gaussian draws with seeds 140000–140063. Each draw is shared across free-volume glucose/insulin/C-peptide (GIC), correctly fixed-volume GIC and glucose/insulin (GI) analyses; pairing prevents a different noise realization from masquerading as a design effect. This gives 192 interior fits. A separate boundary diagnostic uses generating gain `0.5` and the other multipliers one, 32 draws with seeds 240000–240031, and free-volume GIC only. These are 224 planned analyses, not 224 independent people or three independent sets of interior draws. No BC-013 development seed (117 or 221) is reused.

Generate each noiseless 0–240 minute, one-minute-grid trajectory afresh with the unchanged BC-012 reference solver. Add independent Gaussian errors with the inherited engineering SDs of 5 mg/dL glucose, 10 pmol/L insulin and 30 pmol/L C-peptide. Set time-zero glucose/insulin errors exactly zero because those values condition the initial state; retain time-zero C-peptide in the objective. The unchanged 30-minute selection gives 25 GIC or 16 GI residuals. The single synthetic patient, exact basal glucose/insulin, known subcutaneous insulin input and fixed remaining physiology are favorable conditions, not an empirical population distribution.

At each draw and design, fit unrestricted Q from the same three fixed BC-013 starts. Fix **only** gain at its generating value and reoptimize all remaining declared unknowns with BC-013's two deterministic nuisance starts, log-coordinate solver, engineering bounds and exact conditional volume minimization. The third unrestricted start equals the interior generating vector; it is a fixed algorithmic start from BC-013 and a favorable feature of this synthetic setting, not a data-derived estimate. Do not refit SDs, adapt starts to outcome labels or scan complete 27-curve profiles. Compute raw `DeltaQ=Q_fixed_true_gain-Q_unrestricted`; a negative value below `-0.0001` indicates an unresolved minimum and fails the run. Inclusion means raw `DeltaQ <= 3.841458820694124`, including a small recorded negative value within tolerance.

Evaluate both selected fit optima with the reference forward solver for every draw. Each objective and their DeltaQ must agree with the fit solver within `0.001`. Reoptimize both sides with the reference solver when the fitted DeltaQ is within `0.01` of the cutoff or reference evaluation changes the inclusion side. Preserve every start, objective, prediction, residual, nuisance vector, bound hit and solver discrepancy. An unresolved discrepancy is a failure, not a changed cutoff.

## Precision, failures and acceptance

Report included/planned as the primary conservative rate, counting a failed or incomplete analysis in the planned denominator as noncoverage and reporting failures separately. Included/completed is a secondary diagnostic. Use two-sided 95% Wilson score intervals for each planned-denominator proportion; report exact counts and intervals instead of rounding an apparent percentage to 95. At a hypothetical true rate of 0.95, 64 draws give binomial Monte Carlo standard error about 0.027 and approximate normal 95% half-width about 0.053; 32 draws give standard error about 0.039. Actual intervals depend on the observed count. This bounded precision cannot prove exact nominal calibration. The boundary arm has no 95% target under regular asymptotics.

The complete campaign must be run twice in fresh processes with byte-identical observation and numerical case artifacts. A standard-library reader independently reconstructs residual/objective arithmetic, coverage counts, Wilson intervals, all planned denominators and failure accounting; it must reject incomplete or tampered artifacts. Retain the frozen protocol, source snapshots, environment locks, native identity, seeds, all starts and failed checkpoints. Produce an aggregate JSON report and deterministic figure. **Empirical coverage is a result, not a pass/fail gate.** Numerical completion, independent solver checks, exact repetition and artifact reconstruction are the software acceptance gates. Threshold calibration, if later justified, requires separate calibration draws and an untouched future assessment set.

The full campaign may be expensive. A performance measurement using existing BC-013 development draw 117 took about 17 seconds for three free fits plus seven seconds for two true-gain-constrained fits on the local pinned environment; this is planning evidence, not an assessment result or a hosted-runtime estimate. All new assessment seeds remain unread until this protocol is committed.
