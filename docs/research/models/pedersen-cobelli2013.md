# Pedersen–Cobelli reduced IVGTT secretion model

## Identity and provenance

OpenT1D model identifier `pedersen-cobelli-2013-reduced/1`, experiment BC-001/1. Independent implementation of Morten Gram Pedersen and Claudio Cobelli, *Multiscale modelling of insulin secretion during an intravenous glucose tolerance test*, Interface Focus 3(2):20120085 (2013), [DOI 10.1098/rsfs.2012.0085](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsfs.2012.0085). Equations 4.11–4.13 and Figure 2 parameters define the selected reduction; the [dossier](../m4-evidence-dossier.md) maps final/preprint equations and author-code discrepancies.

[Model source](../../../experiments/m4/beta_cell/runtime/model.py) and [parameter/protocol record](../../../experiments/m4/beta_cell/protocol.json) are original OpenT1D implementation/configuration. Author XPPAUT code was inspected, not executed or copied into the repository; no verified redistribution license was found. The upstream granule model has mixed cellular/animal and human illustrative ancestry; this is not a model trained in T1D or transplanted islets. Maintenance is exploratory repository maintenance, with no named scientific reviewer yet.

## Intended use and unsupported claims

Calculate evoked insulin release for a declared single post-bolus declining plasma-glucose profile, characterize initialization and numerical behavior, and prepare questions for domain review. The demonstrator is synthetic. Figure 2c/d theoretical response relationships are reconstructible from published definitions; the original cohort glucose series is unavailable in inspected assets.

The model does not predict glucose, reconstruct secretion from C-peptide, count cells, simulate immunity, assess engraftment or estimate insulin requirements. No meal/incretin effect, rising-glucose derivative response, repeated challenges, pharmacological insulin or chronic capacity dynamics is supported. Extending to a closed glucose loop is a new modeling decision, not a wiring task. No clinical or therapy claim follows from a numerical pass.

## Inputs, states and outputs

| Quantity | Meaning and units |
| --- | --- |
| G(t) | Prescribed plasma glucose, mmol/L; one declining exponential with explicit baseline, initial peak and time constant |
| X | Evoked releasable/fused insulin pool, micrograms |
| Y | Provision to X, micrograms/minute |
| SR = m X | Evoked secretion, micrograms/minute, before any vascular kinetics; excludes basal release |
| Capacity | OpenT1D dimensionless common amplitude multiplier on mobilization and initial X; a hypothetical functional scale |

Time is elapsed minutes from immediately after the idealized bolus. No randomness or persistent state survives a new call. Y starts at zero. X starts from printed equation 4.13 with declared basal D equilibrium; an alternative run exposes the author's 92.7-microgram initialization. M0 is basal mobilization and must not be interpreted as basal secretion. The [protocol](../m4-protocol.md) records all rates, dimensions, initialization and the exact synthetic input. No clearance, hepatic extraction or C-peptide process is owned here; [coupling](../m4-coupling.md) remains proposed.

## Uncertainty, failures and rights

The subsequent [BC-006 source audit](../m4-source-audit-results.md) identifies whole-pancreas intent and the separately coded basal term. A pre-bolus-pool hypothesis explains the explicit 92.7-microgram value to its reported precision, while a separate C-peptide amplitude multiplier and warmup-state uncertainty prevent an author-workflow reproduction. The audit quantifies the product-rule and Y-initialization distinctions. These are internal deductions, not an erratum or a change to this version; population-specific scale/basal calibration and external review remain open.

The printed and coded initial burst differ materially. The reduction also omits the derivative term generated by the glucose-dependent change of variables; it is reproduced as the published approximation. These limitations constrain interpretation even when integration is numerically precise. Capacity is confounded with common amplitude scaling; human parameter identifiability remains untested. Sparse or noisy C-peptide cannot automatically recover all granule parameters.

Invalid units/configuration, nonfinite quantities, invalid time grids and incomplete solver returns fail explicitly. A failed run retains its configuration and error, and artifact readers reject failed, missing, altered or incomplete results. No missing input is silently replaced by a person, basal rate or fit.
