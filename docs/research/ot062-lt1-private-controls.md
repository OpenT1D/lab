## Host evidence and qualification

The earlier [four scripted host cases](ot062-lt1-host-qualification.md) establish
their stated fixture observations. They do not qualify a native Node profile,
whole bootstrap/stdlib/dylib TCB, hostile owner, arbitrary IPC or computed
module/addon closure. The current worker rejects every `execArgv`; permission or
no-addon flags need a reviewed successor and cannot be assumed available.

New read-only records remain private. Current host/session metadata matches the
private use decision. Known private evidence roots have scoped owner/access
observations; one historical root's broader permissions were tightened. Scoped
Time Machine exclusion queries succeeded. Root ACL/exclusion observations do not
prove whole-host ACL or snapshot policy. Further snapshot/backup queries returned
success, while FileVault/volume-detail queries were unavailable. Those gaps
provide no encryption, volume or effective-erasure clearance.

Before actual native host admission, independently review and qualify:

1. Current host/OS, runtime/source/config/reader/tools and complete trusted startup
   paths, including initialization, environment/preloads and change withdrawal.
   Static hashes alone do not qualify a bootstrap.
2. Storage ownership/inherited ACLs, aliases/hardlinks and writable/map-executable
   boundaries, with exact scratch/temp/log and input locations.
3. Native file/network/relevant IPC, module/addon and executable-spawn allow/deny
   observations. Include successful allowed/unconfined controls. Missing targets
   and connection refusal do not prove denial; review broad mach/bootstrap grants.
4. Actual native children/grandchildren through the existing process owner,
   unrelated sentinel preservation, start identities, escalation, deadline/cancel,
   coordinator loss and empty final ownership. Native C15–C18 and trusted durable
   reconstruction retain their separate criteria.
5. Complete copy/recovery coverage and enforceable retention, including offline
   backups and snapshots. Unqualified storage blocks setup despite private modes.

These prerequisites do not assert that the native cases ran.

## Read-only retention planning

[ADR 0098](../decisions/0098-private-retention-planning.md) defines
[retention.py](../../experiments/lt1_diagnostic/retention.py). Bounded inputs are
an externally reviewed register, separate approved policy, current context and
known predecessor. Opaque subject/copy IDs map to exact private paths in separate
reviewed evidence. Publish neither that map nor actual policy/personal hashes.
Expected identities require an independent trusted source; recomputing them from
unreviewed inputs does not establish authority or factual evidence.
The caller also supplies the actual reviewed UTC observation time. The planner
does not discover a clock, missing predecessor chain or hidden filesystem copies.

Original audit creation/acquisition evidence establishes each subject's clock.
Copies/revisions inherit it; independently generated outputs have new subjects.
Rename, relocation, mtime, birth time or updating a record does not establish a
new origin. Known subjects/copies cannot disappear, established origins cannot
move forward, and a predecessor chain cannot change policy. This prevents silent
deadline extension. Complete history, authentic origins and correct subject
classification still require external review.

The planner returns exact inclusive UTC deadlines, due copy IDs and blockers for
unknown origins, incomplete coverage, holds and recovery beyond expiry. Changed
bytes, stale context and unsupported schema refuse. Expired reportedly absent
copies keep an explicit expiry/review status. It opens no private file and makes
no deletion, scheduling, receipt or admission change. Effective erasure and every
authority stay false. Due actions in blocked plans are review items only.
`recovery_scope_reviewed: false` always blocks. A null `recoverable_until` with
reviewed scope means an explicit evidenced assertion that no applicable recovery
deadline remains; it must never substitute for unknown backup/snapshot coverage.

The [invented tests](../../tests/test_lt1_retention.py) use an illustrative
seven-day policy. They cover expiry boundaries, copy/revision clocks, independent
outputs, policy/origin/history preservation, missing coverage, backup/hold limits,
absent copies, context/schema/date/flag refusals and bounded intake. Separate
review found and corrected policy-driven deadline extension and a misleading
expired/absent status. No actual private file or native artifact is a test input.

A separate private pilot returned a blocked plan for an unestablished original
clock and incomplete copy/recovery/process coverage. It assumed no origin from
filesystem times, exposed no personal record and performed no deletion. This is
an observed refusal, not a complete retention qualification.

## Manual removal and verification

1. Stop the audit and establish that no owned writer/reader/descendant remains.
   Retain actual process-owner observations and preserve unrelated processes.
   A register's claimed stop flag is not this evidence.
2. Freeze exact subjects/copies and predecessor history. Inventory payloads,
   personal decisions, raw receipts, logs, scratch/temp/cache data, relocated or
   revised records and recovery locations. Resolve unknown origins and coverage
   before actual setup. Holds conflicting with expiry require a decision; they
   do not extend the approved maximum.
3. Review backups/snapshots for these exact objects. Apple states that
   [Time Machine exclusions do not exclude local snapshots](https://support.apple.com/en-au/guide/mac-help/mh15622/27/mac/27).
   Its [snapshot guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102154) also describes
   last-successful-backup and update snapshots beyond ordinary hourly cases.
   Exclusion or one empty query does not establish a fixed recovery bound. Any
   future isolated encrypted storage needs a separate key/recovery/backup review;
   no container, key or backup configuration is created here.
4. At expiry, review an exact removal inventory and operation. Recheck current
   host/source/context, canonical approved roots, directory bindings, UID/modes/
   ACLs and ordinary single-link file identities. Refuse links, special files,
   unlisted/changed entries and namespace changes. Do not follow links, adopt new
   roots or use unrestricted recursive removal. This increment ships no general
   deletion CLI and removes no existing evidence, backup or snapshot.
5. Observe named-path absence after process cleanup, then verify every registered
   copy/recovery location and deadline separately. Do not label path absence as
   effective erasure while recovery or evidence gaps remain. Preserve partial
   failure status without resetting origins or silently dropping records.
6. Any retained completion record is itself private, with an approved purpose
   and limit. Do not create a permanent personal identifier or publish a digest
   of a deleted personal record. Keep exports closed and resolve any retention
   conflict with the decision owner.

The existing-evidence inventory covers known private roots only. Original clocks
and global copy/temp/cache/log/backup coverage remain incomplete. Native host
admission and effective erasure remain unqualified. Historical locks/guards,
scientific readers, baselines/tolerances, publication scope and all production
admissions are unchanged.

The [host/removal successor](ot062-lt1-host-removal.md) implements separate exact
quarantine/removal authority and explicit invented boundary/process fixtures.
It qualifies neither actual private cleanup nor effective recovery/physical erasure;
the manual procedure and all existing setup/execution gates remain in force.

## One leaf in a mixed historical directory

[ADR 0113](../decisions/0113-lt1-historical-leaf-removal.md) selects an additive
[historical-leaf helper](../../experiments/lt1_diagnostic/historical_leaf_removal.py).
The flat remover's closed all-file inventory and native tree remover's fixed
roles remain unchanged. A historical directory containing unrelated files and
subdirectories cannot be represented by approving its whole contents just to
remove one record.

The versioned request identifies exactly one ordinary single-link target, its
private canonical parent, every reviewed immediate sibling and a separate private
same-volume evidence parent. Bind target bytes and metadata, both directory
identities, current implementation/host and externally reviewed context. The
bounded sibling inventory is nonrecursive metadata: it grants no sibling or
descendant deletion and does not prove descendant bytes unchanged. Links,
special files and unsupported namespaces refuse rather than becoming traversal
instructions. ACLs, exclusive process ownership and full storage/recovery scope
still require separate review; mode bits do not establish them.

The production entry checks the exact raw request's membership in the existing
canonical removal registry before request/path intake. No alternative registry,
automatic approval or general recursive cleanup command is added. The registry
remains empty. Re-read its live membership before moving, unlinking and recording
completion. Revocation is not ignored merely because the operation previously
passed admission: retain prepared/quarantined evidence and stop further mutation.
Historical readback does not restore or require the old live grant. Tests inject
authority only for freshly invented objects.

Canonical spellings alone do not establish separate physical roles. On macOS,
APFS firmlinks can name the same directory through different canonical paths.
Use opened directory identities and bounded descriptor-relative parent walks to
reject identical root/evidence objects and either physical ancestor relationship
before target intake or evidence creation. Recheck those roles in transitions
and readback; sharing only a common ancestor is valid for disjoint roots.

Quarantine only the pinned target through an exclusive same-volume rename. Hold
and recheck descriptors, exact bytes, source, journal, directories and immediate
sibling identities before moving and before the irreversible unlink. Refresh
target bytes and held/path binding after the last source check, and refresh
source/directory roles after long initial reads before creating evidence.
New entries, replacements,
changed bindings and collisions refuse. Cancellation and crash gaps retain their
actual journal and target/quarantine observations; they do not resume or infer a
missing mutation event. The exclusive-owner assumption remains essential because
POSIX unlink cannot atomically compare the expected inode against a hostile
same-UID writer.

The reader consumes externally pinned request, journal and actual owned outer
receipt bytes. The reviewed context binds the expected command; a concrete
supervisor receipt must preserve that command and process start identities.
External byte pins do not authenticate a producer: the reviewer must establish
the trusted producer and its correspondence to this operation independently.
Completion requires a coherent finished journal, successful process cleanup,
current target absence and the reviewed immediate sibling state.
An incomplete process, missing event, changed evidence or mismatched external pin
cannot become a completed result merely by resealing inner data. Completion
describes the target namespace only. No setup, native execution, new authority,
timely retention or effective-erasure clearance follows.

The private copy/recovery follow-up distinguishes literal personal-record copies
from preexisting source attribution and public commit metadata. Bounded passive
reads may narrow actual text, binary, bytecode and archive exclusions; link
targets and source/predecessor references need their own explicit accounting.
Owner reports remain separate from collected observations. Current empty
snapshot/destination queries, exclusions and volume encryption do not establish
historical/offline coverage, future recovery limits or per-file key destruction.
Original clocks and holds remain unchanged, and newly retained proof files are
copies or new subjects requiring their own bounded retention. Actual paths,
identities, host values and policy hashes stay outside Git.

The [focused tests](../../tests/test_lt1_historical_leaf_removal.py) and
[process fixtures](../../tests/test_lt1_historical_leaf_process.py) use invented
data only. Actual native host qualification and removal of existing private
records remain behind their separate gates. An unknown original origin prevents
a timely-retention claim; it does not permanently prohibit an independently
reviewed, separately admitted exact known-copy cleanup.
