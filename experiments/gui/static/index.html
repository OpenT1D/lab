RETROSPECTIVE OBSERVATION ANALYSIS

Analyze paired meal tests

Compare a paired secretion conclusion across explicitly frozen assumptions.

Invented observations only

Whole-body total endogenous secretion, including basal, over measured 0–120 minutes. Change is follow-up minus baseline in pmol. C-peptide AUC and glucose context remain separate.

1. Choose observations

Use your own synthetic CSV and metadata

Select files inside this checkout. Metadata declares participant/visit linkage, assay limits and context. CSV uses minutes, mmol/L glucose and nmol/L C-peptide. Human declarations are refused.

2. Review and freeze the plan

Review or change assumptions

One fixed-kinetic two-compartment method, exact interval integration and no automatic weight, BSA or glucose normalization. Factor 1 means initial compartment equilibrium; other inventory factors are declared stresses.

Conditional synthetic measurement uncertainty

Invented Gaussian controls, not measured assay precision or a human uncertainty distribution. One offset is shared across both visits; every draw recalculates initialization.

Review assumptions before freezing.

A freeze preserves the exact input bytes and these settings before evaluation. Editing creates a new plan. Failed observations and variants remain explicit; nothing is silently imputed or clipped.
