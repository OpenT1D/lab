# Code of conduct

OpenT1D is a place for people with different scientific backgrounds, technical skills and experiences of type 1 diabetes to work together. Participation must be respectful, including when people disagree strongly about an idea or result.

## Expected behavior

- Address the work and evidence. Explain objections without attacking the person presenting them.
- Make room for questions, corrections and differences in experience, language and accessibility needs.
- Credit other people's contributions and respect their privacy.
- Respect a request to stop unwanted contact and follow reasonable moderation instructions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, deliberate humiliation, impersonation and publishing someone's private information without permission are unacceptable. Repeated disruption or targeted hostility is also unacceptable. Scientific criticism and good-faith disagreement are welcome.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests and other spaces operated by OpenT1D. It also applies when someone explicitly represents the project elsewhere. It does not make maintainers responsible for every unrelated interaction between participants.

## Reporting

Send confidential conduct reports to [Matthiasvdgalien97@gmail.com](mailto:Matthiasvdgalien97@gmail.com). Reports are handled by the project maintainer. Do not post a confidential report in a public issue. For content that violates GitHub's rules, GitHub also provides a [reporting route](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

Reports should describe the behavior, where it occurred and any steps needed to limit further contact. Share only the personal information necessary to assess the report. Reports are handled as privately as practical; maintainers cannot promise absolute confidentiality where platform intervention or other obligations require disclosure.

## Response and review

The maintainer may remove content, ask someone to change their behavior, restrict participation temporarily or exclude a participant. The response depends on severity, repetition and impact; serious incidents can require immediate action. Where appropriate, explain the decision privately and offer a chance to correct factual errors.

The [governance document](GOVERNANCE.md) identifies the current maintainer and the limitations of a single-maintainer project. A report involving that maintainer has no independent internal adjudicator at present. Participants may use GitHub's reporting process for platform violations. An independent project contact will be added when one is appointed.
